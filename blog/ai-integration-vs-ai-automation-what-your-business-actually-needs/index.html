AI Integration vs AI Automation: What Your Business Actually Needs

A clear breakdown of when you need AI integration, when you need automation, and how to scope both correctly.

ai integrationautomationscope planningworkflow design

Different Problems

AI integration connects intelligence into existing software. Automation moves data and actions across tools. Many projects need both, but not in the same phase or at the same cost.

Where Integration Fits

Use integration when the team needs model-assisted search, summarization, classification, or generation inside the systems they already use.

Where Automation Fits

Use automation when the work is repetitive, rule-driven, and spread across systems like CRM, billing, email, and scheduling.

How to Scope Correctly

Pick one business outcome, map the manual workflow, identify the AI decision points, and define the handoff points that should remain human-led.
