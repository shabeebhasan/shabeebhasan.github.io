Voice Explorer: a Talent Showcase App on Android (University Project)

My final-year university project: an Android app to record a performance, trim and merge it on the phone with FFmpeg, upload it to YouTube and share it, with an admin app and an AngularJS web app.

androidffmpegyoutube data apiparseangularjs

The problem

New singers and performers had no simple way to record something decent on a phone, clean it up, and put it in front of people in one place.

What I built

  • Record audio or video in the app.
  • Edit on the phone: trim with a range slider, merge audio with video and change the resolution with FFmpeg.
  • Upload to YouTube through the YouTube Data API with resumable uploads, so a dropped connection does not restart the upload.
  • Discover new talent, tracks and videos, and chat and call other users.
  • Admin app and web app: an Android admin app and an AngularJS web app with social sign-in.

Stack

Android (Java), FFmpeg, YouTube Data API, Parse, Facebook SDK, AngularJS
