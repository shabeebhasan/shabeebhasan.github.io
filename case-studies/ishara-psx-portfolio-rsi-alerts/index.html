Ishara: A Live PSX Portfolio Tracker That Tested Its Own Rule First

My own product. It values your Pakistan Stock Exchange holdings through the trading day and sends a short notice when a stock's RSI crosses a level. Before building any alert, I tested the rule on five years of data. It did not beat simply holding the stocks, so the app says that on every page.

What It Does

You paste in your holdings. Ishara prices them every 60 seconds while the market is open, compares each purchase with the KSE-100 from the day you bought it, and keeps five years of history, dividends and company reports in one place. When a stock you follow crosses an RSI level on the daily close, you get a short notice on your dashboard and, if you want, on your phone.

It never connects to a broker and it cannot place an order. That is on purpose.

The Honest Part

The first idea was a momentum rule: buy when RSI goes above 70. Before writing a single alert, I tested it on five years of daily prices for 39 liquid PSX stocks. It lost to simply holding the same stocks on 34 of the 39. Tuning did not save it: ninety combinations were tried on one period, and every winner fell apart on a second period it had never seen.

The reverse rule, buying when RSI drops through 40, held up much better. It kept a win rate near 68% on the unseen period. But it still did not clearly beat holding the stocks: 25.7% against 27.5%.

So Ishara calls these notices, never signals. The evidence is a page inside the app, and the same warning sits in the footer of every page and at the end of every alert. A product that dressed this up as advice would be claiming something its own test contradicts.

How It Is Built

  • FastAPI and one SQLite file. No queue, no cache, no build step. Two processes share the database: the web app and a scheduler.
  • One module talks to the market data source. If the source changes, one file changes, not the whole app.
  • Price history is cleaned before RSI is worked out. Splits and bonus shares are adjusted, and where a jump cannot be explained, RSI restarts instead of carrying a false spike.
  • Alerts go to the phone through ntfy. Each account picks its own topic. Nothing needs to be installed on the server for it.
  • Light and dark themes, and it works on a phone.

Why It Matters for Your Project

Most dashboards show numbers. This one also tells you when a number should not be trusted. If you are building a finance app, a trading tool or anything that turns data into a decision, that is the part that protects you and your users: test the idea before you build around it, and say plainly what the test found.
