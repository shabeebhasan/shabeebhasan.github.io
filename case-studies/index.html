Two real builds that show how product goals were translated into production engineering.

Case Study 01

Depression and Anxiety Detector

Problem: Mental-health screening is often delayed and inaccessible in many markets.

Solution: Built a computer-vision system for real-time and recorded-video facial expression analysis using Python, OpenCV, and TensorFlow.

Outcome: Production-ready ML workflow for telehealth and wellness product integration.

Tech Stack: Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow/Keras, scikit-learn

Use Cases: Telehealth startups, wellness apps, HR wellbeing systems, EdTech support

Case Study 02

Roam Reality AR Travel Platform

Problem: Traditional travel apps are static and fail to deliver immersive destination experiences.

Solution: Designed a mobile AR platform with geolocation-aware overlays, gamified interactions, and leaderboards using React Native and ViroReact.

Outcome: A strong mobile-first concept ready for tourism, city-guide, and experiential campaigns.

Tech Stack: React Native, ViroReact, GPS/Geolocation APIs, 3D assets

Use Cases: Tourism boards, hospitality startups, destination marketing teams
