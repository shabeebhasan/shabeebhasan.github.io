Food Delivery Platform: Customer App, Driver App, Admin and Django API

A three-sided food delivery product built on contract from June 2020 to May 2021: a React Native customer app, a React Native driver app, a React admin for restaurants, and a Django REST backend with Stripe Connect payouts and PostGIS locations. Six milestones.

react nativedjango rest frameworkreactstripe connectpostgisfood delivery

What it is

A delivery product with three sides. Customers order food from nearby restaurants. Drivers accept and deliver the orders. Restaurants manage their menu, orders and drivers from a web admin. All three run on one Django REST API.

The work was a contract with six milestones between June 2020 and May 2021.

Customer app

  • Restaurant search, menu categories, and items with sizes, add-ons and reviews.
  • A cart, then a three-step checkout: contact details, card, and place order.
  • Card payments through Stripe. The app checks the card number, expiry and CVV before sending anything.
  • Order history, a star rating and review after each order, chat, and push notifications.

Driver app

  • New orders arrive by push. The driver accepts or rejects them, then marks them delivered.
  • A map with the delivery location and the full order.
  • Delivery history, chat with the customer, and a profile with vehicle, licence and bank details.

Restaurant admin

  • React with CoreUI, Redux and redux-saga.
  • Orders grouped as Pending, Chef Assigned, Ongoing and Delivered, with search. Drivers are assigned from here.
  • Menu items with sizes and add-ons, with drag-and-drop images. Also driver accounts and monthly sales charts.

Backend

  • Django and Django REST Framework, with separate apps for restaurants, orders, drivers and users.
  • Stripe Connect: each restaurant has its own connected account and is paid out directly. Webhooks handle the payment events.
  • PostGIS for restaurant and delivery locations.
  • Order endpoints to add, accept, decline and mark orders delivered. FCM push to both apps.
  • Google and Apple sign-in. Deployed with Docker on Heroku.
