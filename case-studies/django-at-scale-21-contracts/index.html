The Brief

A US platform company builds products for many end-clients at once on a standardised Django + React stack. Engineers are engaged per product, and the ones who deliver get the next one. They engaged me 21 separate times over six years.

What the Work Looked Like

  • Full-stack Django/DRF product builds (models, APIs, auth, admin) under a shared platform architecture where consistency beats cleverness.
  • Parallel delivery: multiple product contracts running side by side, each with its own client expectations, on the same standards.
  • Range within one stack: dashboards, mobile backends, deployment work, and a 4-year Django interactive-forms engagement for another client alongside.
  • The same discipline now powers francofunrewards.ca (Django 5 in production) and FastAPI runtimes for AI systems.
