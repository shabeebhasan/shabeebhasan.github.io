ColorBook: an iOS Colouring App with a Hand-Written Flood Fill

An iOS colouring app for detailed line art. A tap fills an area with colour using a scanline flood fill written in Objective-C and called from Swift.

iosswiftobjective-cimage processing

The problem

Detailed mandala and animal line art has thousands of small regions. A simple recursive fill is slow on full-screen images and can crash on large areas.

What I built

  • Scanline flood fill in Objective-C: each row is filled left and right in one pass, and the rows above and below go onto a linked-list stack instead of the call stack, so big areas never overflow.
  • Tolerance, so anti-aliased line edges fill cleanly.
  • Tap mapping from the zoomable view to the bitmap pixel, so the fill lands where the finger is.
  • Palette of colours plus a picker for custom colours.

Stack

Swift, Objective-C, UIKit, Core Graphics
